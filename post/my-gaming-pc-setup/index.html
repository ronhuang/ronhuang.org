My Gaming PC Setup (2021 Build)

    Ron Huang
  • 25th Nov 2025
  • •Tags: 
  • gaming

I was tired of digging through my email for the parts list, so here’s the permanent home for my gaming rig.

I find myself constantly checking the specs of my gaming PC. The details were originally kept in an email and always took me a while to locate. Although I have had this PC since 2021, I thought I should make it into a blog post for easier future reference.

This build was put together with a focus on quiet performance and a relatively small footprint on my desk. It is not the absolute top-of-the-line anymore, but it comfortably handles modern AAA titles at 1440p with high settings.

ComponentName
CPUAMD Ryzen 9 5900X
CPU CoolerNoctua NH-C14S
MotherboardGigabyte B550I AORUS PRO AX
MemoryCrucial Ballistix 32 GB (2 x 16 GB) DDR4-3600 CL16
StorageWestern Digital Black SN750 1 TB M.2-2280
Video CardEVGA GeForce RTX 3080 XC3 ULTRA GAMING 10 GB
CaseCooler Master MasterBox NR200 Mini-ITX
Power SupplyCooler Master V750 SFX Gold 750W

It has lasted almost five years now and still runs pretty smoothly. I am curious to see how long it will be before I feel the need for a major upgrade, but for now this setup continues to be more than enough for both gaming and everyday use.
