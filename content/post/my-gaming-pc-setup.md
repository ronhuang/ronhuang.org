I find myself constantly checking the specs of my gaming PC. The details were
originally kept in an email and always took me a while to locate. Although I
have had this PC since 2021, I thought I should make it into a blog post for
easier future reference.

This build was put together with a focus on quiet performance and a relatively
small footprint on my desk. It is not the absolute top-of-the-line anymore, but
it comfortably handles modern AAA titles at 1440p with high settings.

| Component    | Name                                               |
|--------------|----------------------------------------------------|
| CPU          | AMD Ryzen 9 5900X                                  |
| CPU Cooler   | Noctua NH-C14S                                     |
| Motherboard  | Gigabyte B550I AORUS PRO AX                        |
| Memory       | Crucial Ballistix 32 GB (2 x 16 GB) DDR4-3600 CL16 |
| Storage      | Western Digital Black SN750 1 TB M.2-2280          |
| Video Card   | EVGA GeForce RTX 3080 XC3 ULTRA GAMING 10 GB       |
| Case         | Cooler Master MasterBox NR200 Mini-ITX             |
| Power Supply | Cooler Master V750 SFX Gold 750W                   |

It has lasted almost five years now and still runs pretty smoothly. I am curious
to see how long it will be before I feel the need for a major upgrade, but for
now this setup continues to be more than enough for both gaming and everyday
use.
